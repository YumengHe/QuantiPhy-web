Why PhysMind performs poorly

  • Domain shift is part of the reason: PhysMind is designed for CLEVRER-style synthetic scenes and does best on the simulation videos (60% measurable, MRA 0.398); real videos are worse (captured 54% / 0.280, internet 52% / 0.260). Parts and static structures have no mesh and cannot be measured.
  • The main reason is that it does not use the prior: the scale comes from monocular depth alone and has large errors (median prediction / GT 1.13, but only 50 of 89 within a factor of 2); on the same questions, even on simulation it loses to Gemini answering directly (0.398 vs 0.518).
  • 15 questions (2 videos) where SAM 3 found no object: simulation_0193 (none of its 9 object names found) and internet_0001 ("black car"); the later annotation step fails and the answer falls back to the VLM.
