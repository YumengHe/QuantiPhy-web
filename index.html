Reconstructing measurable physical scenes from video

Given a video, reconstruct the scene at real-world scale: what the objects are, how large they are, where they are and how they move. Quantitative questions about size, speed and distance are then measured directly in the reconstructed scene.

Keywords: Minimal Sufficient Reconstruction

Pipeline

The prior, the known facts a question gives (an object's size or speed, or its distance to the camera), sets the scale. Every video runs the same pipeline and is fitted as a 3D scene: focal length and support-plane pose are estimated from the image, and objects have thickness. Every measurement of the asked quantity in the scene is kept with its uncertainty and named checks; a value becomes the answer only when the measurements agree and every check passes, otherwise the question stays unresolved.

Objects→Scene graph (grid)→Observe→ Track→Measure→Scene graph (per frame)→ Ground→Physics-guided Fitting→ Agent loop→Answer
