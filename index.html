Reconstructing measurable physical scenes from video

Given a video, reconstruct the scene at real-world scale: what the objects are, how large they are, where they are and how they move. Quantitative questions about size, speed and distance are then measured directly in the reconstructed scene.

Pipeline

The prior, the known facts a question gives (an object's size or speed, or its distance to the camera), sets the scale. Every video runs the same pipeline and is fitted as a 3D scene: camera, ground and objects. Planar videos, whose prior has no distance anchor, use the same optimizer with a planar camera: nominal focal length, a support plane facing the camera, and a plane depth derived from the prior's scale. Questions the scene cannot answer are estimated by the model and marked prior.

Object list→scene graph→Tracking→ Observations→Physics-guided Fitting→ agent loop (2–10 rounds)→Answer from the scene
